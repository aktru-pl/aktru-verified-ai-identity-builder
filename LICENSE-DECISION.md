# License decision

## Selected license

The project owner selected the MIT License for the public release.

Copyright notice:

`Copyright (c) 2026 AKTRU Sp. z o.o.`

The complete standard license text is included in `LICENSE`.

## Rationale

MIT is short, familiar and permissive. It allows use, copying, modification, distribution, sublicensing and commercial reuse while requiring preservation of the copyright and permission notice. It supports straightforward judging, public inspection and future adoption with a low compliance burden.

## Accepted trade-offs

MIT permits proprietary derivatives and does not include the explicit patent-license language found in Apache-2.0. The owner accepted these trade-offs for this release.
